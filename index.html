Hearthforge

A medieval fantasy action RPG in a world built of blocks. Build and survive, fight with lock-on, rolls and parries, train twenty-three skills, tame creatures, raise a settlement and explore dungeons, alone or with friends.

Play

Hearthforge runs on Windows (desktop app), Android and in the browser. Play alone, together on one screen with up to four players, or online with friends.

Accounts

An account is optional. With one you can keep your characters in the cloud, play them on any device, add friends, send messages and join guilds. You can sign in with an email address and password or with Google.

Read how your information is handled in the Privacy Policy, and the rules for using the game in the Terms of Service.
