Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information Hearthforge ("the game", "we") collects, why, where it is kept and what choices you have. It applies to the Windows desktop app, the Android app and the browser version.

Playing without an account

You can play without an account. Your worlds, characters and settings are then stored only on your own device (in the app's storage or your browser's storage). We do not receive them.

What we collect when you create an account

  • Sign-in details: your email address. If you use email and password, your password is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we never see it. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address and basic profile (name and picture) and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password or access to any other Google data.
  • Account name you choose, the date the account was made and when it was last used.
  • Characters and cloud saves: the characters you keep in the cloud and their saved progress, so you can play them on another device.
  • Ranks earned in the game's competitive modes.
  • Friends and blocks: your friend code, your friends list, friend requests and the players you have blocked.
  • Online status shown to your friends (for example that you are online and which character you are playing). You can hide it in the game's Friends window.
  • Messages: direct messages you send to friends and messages in guild chat, and the guilds you found or join.
  • Reports you make about another player, and reports others make about you, with the reason and the details given.
  • Technical records: short-lived codes that confirm who is joining an online game, and basic error and event records (game version, platform and what went wrong) that help us fix problems.

Bug reports and suggestions

If you send a bug report or suggestion from inside the game, it is posted to the game's community server on Discord. It contains what you write, your character's name and, unless you untick them, a screenshot of the game and technical details (game version, platform and browser or device information, graphics settings and recent errors). Do not include personal information you do not want shared there.

Playing online with others

When you play online, your game connects to other players' games, partly through public relay services that pass connection messages along. Players you play with see your character, its name and what it does in the game. As with most online games, the devices you connect to can see your internet (IP) address.

How we use your information

  • To let you sign in, keep your characters in the cloud and play them on your devices.
  • To provide friends, messages, guilds, online play and ranks.
  • To keep the game safe and fair: enforce blocks, review reports and act on abuse or cheating.
  • To find and fix bugs and keep the game running.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers below, which store or carry it for us.

Service providers

  • Supabase: account sign-in and the database that holds the account information above.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Cloudflare: hosts the browser version of the game.
  • GitHub: hosts game downloads and updates (the desktop app checks it for new versions).
  • Discord: receives in-game bug reports and suggestions.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept while your account exists. Connection codes expire within minutes. Error records are kept only as long as they are useful for fixing problems. When an account is deleted, its characters, cloud saves, friends, blocks and memberships are deleted with it; messages you sent to other players may stay in their conversations without your account attached.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without an account, and you can sign out at any time.
  • You can hide your online status from friends, remove friends and block players in the game.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, for it to be corrected, or for your account and its information to be deleted, by emailing CONTACT_EMAIL from the email address on the account (or telling us your account name and friend code). We answer within 30 days.

Children

The online account features are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and the database only lets each player read and change what belongs to them. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date. Important changes are also announced in the game's release notes.
